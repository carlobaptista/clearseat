Facts first. Then a plan. Then someone to carry it out.

We work the way a good finance director would: establish what is true, agree what matters, then report against it every month.

  1. A 30-minute call

    You talk, we listen. Bring your frustrations and a rough list of suppliers. If we are not the right fit, we will say so and suggest who might be.

  2. The IT Health Check

    Over three weeks we collect your contracts and invoices, speak to your suppliers and a few of your staff, and test the basics. It takes about two hours of your own time.

  3. A one-page plan

    We walk you through what we found, in order of importance: what to fix, what to renegotiate, what to stop paying for. The plan is yours. See a sample.

  4. The seat, filled

    If you want it carried out, we agree a flat monthly fee. From then on, IT has an owner, and you get a short report every quarter.

What you can expect from us.

  • Independence

    We are paid only by our clients. We have nothing to sell you except our time and judgment, so our advice is never shaped by a margin.

  • Plain language

    You will not get a 60-page report. You will get one page you can act on, and straight answers to your questions.

  • Fixed fees

    The health check is a fixed price. Monthly work is a flat fee agreed in advance. You never pay by the hour.

  • Senior attention

    The person you meet on the first call is the person who does the work and signs off every finding.

  • Remote first

    We work by video call, phone and shared documents, which keeps costs down and means we can serve branches anywhere. We visit when being in the room matters.

  • Modern tools, human judgment

    We use AI tools to read contracts and invoices faster and more thoroughly than a person could alone. Carlo checks and stands behind every conclusion.

  • Care with your information

    We collect only what the work needs, keep it secure, and delete or return it when the work ends. We handle personal information in line with POPIA.

What we need from you.

  • About two hours of the owner's or managing director's time during the health check
  • Copies of your supplier contracts and the last three months of invoices
  • An introduction to each supplier, so they know we are acting for you
  • One person in the business we can ask practical questions
