IT Health Check

Once-off fee R45,000, fixed. Three weeks.

The place to start. A complete, independent picture of your IT: what you have, what it costs, where it is exposed and what to do first.

Who it's for

Owners who suspect they are overpaying or under-protected, and want facts before they change anything.

What's included

  • A register of every system, supplier, contract, renewal date and monthly cost
  • A plain-English review of your contracts and service agreements, including notice periods and price increases
  • Checks on the basics: backups, who has access, software updates and what happens if a branch goes offline
  • A review of phone and internet costs across all sites
  • A one-page plan in priority order, and a 60-minute session to walk you through it

What you get

A clear list of what to fix, what to renegotiate and what to stop paying for, with the savings set against our fee. It is yours to act on with or without us. See a sample report.

ClearSeat Advisor

Monthly fee From R20,000 a month.

A senior IT leader on call for your decisions, and a regular eye on your suppliers and renewals.

Who it's for

Businesses whose IT mostly runs, but who want someone experienced to check suppliers, contracts and plans before money is spent.

What's included

  • A monthly working session with you or your management team
  • Review of any new contract, quote or renewal before you sign
  • A quarterly check that suppliers are delivering what they agreed
  • Your register of systems, contracts and costs kept up to date
  • Answers by email or phone when a decision can't wait

What you get

Fewer surprises, better deals and a second opinion you can trust because it has nothing to sell.

ClearSeat Partner

Monthly fee From R45,000 a month.

The full seat. ClearSeat acts as your head of IT: setting the plan, managing the suppliers and reporting to you on progress.

Who it's for

Growing businesses with several sites and several suppliers, where IT problems are now costing management time every week.

What's included

  • Everything in Advisor
  • A 12-month technology plan and budget, agreed with you
  • Day-to-day management of your IT suppliers, including regular service reviews and escalations
  • Supplier selection and negotiation when something needs replacing
  • Oversight of your IT projects
  • A short quarterly report for owners: what was done, what it cost, what is next

What you get

IT stops landing on your desk. One person owns it, and you get a regular report that says where things stand.

Project leadership

Quoted per project Quoted per project.

When something big has to change, we run it as a proper project so that it lands on time and the business keeps trading.

Who it's for

Businesses opening or moving branches, replacing a till, booking or accounting system, or changing a major supplier.

What's included

  • A clear scope, plan, budget and set of risks before work starts
  • Supplier selection and contract negotiation
  • Weekly coordination of everyone involved, yours and theirs
  • Testing, staff training and a planned switch-over
  • A written close-out: what was delivered and what the supplier still owes you

What you get

The change is delivered once, properly, without the usual months of drift.

AI and automation starter

Quoted Quoted after a call.

A practical first step with AI. We find the repetitive work in your business that software can now do, set one process up and measure the time saved.

Who it's for

Owners who have tried the tools and want real results, not experiments.

What's included

  • A review of where staff time goes on repetitive admin

What you get

One working improvement, a measured saving and a short list of what to do next.

  • We don't lock you into long contracts.
