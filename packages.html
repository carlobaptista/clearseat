Questions owners ask.

Why "from"?

A three-branch business with four suppliers needs less of our time than a twelve-branch group with ten. After the health check we know exactly what is involved and give you one fixed monthly figure.

Do I have to start with the health check?

Almost always, yes. Without it we would be advising on guesswork, and you would be paying a monthly fee before either of us knew what needed doing.

Will you replace my IT support company?

Not unless they are failing you. Most of the time the right answer is the same supplier, managed properly and held to a clear agreement.

How do you stay independent?

We are paid only by you. We sell no products, resell no services and accept no commission or referral fees. If we recommend a supplier, it is because they fit, and you choose from more than one quote.

Will it pay for itself?

That is the aim. We start with your contracts and invoices because that is where money hides: unused licences, lines nobody needs, renewals that quietly went up. We set what we find against our fee in the report, so you can judge for yourself. We can't promise a figure before we have seen your contracts, and we won't pretend otherwise.
