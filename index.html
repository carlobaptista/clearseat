Independent by design. We sell no hardware, software or connectivity, and we take no commission from anyone who does.

If you run several branches, this will sound familiar.

ClearSeat is built for owner-managed businesses with 30 to 300 people, more than one site and no IT manager. Salon and clinic groups, restaurants, retailers, practices and franchises.

  • Five suppliers, and nobody in charge.

    Tills, bookings, phones, internet and the IT support company all come from different people. When something breaks, each one points at another, and you become the referee.

  • Systems that don't talk to each other.

    Numbers get retyped from one system into a spreadsheet. Month-end depends on the one person who knows how it all fits together.

  • Bills you can't explain.

    Contracts renew on their own. You suspect you are paying for things you no longer use, but there is never time to check.

  • Security taken on trust.

    You assume the backups work and that people who left can no longer log in. Nobody has tested either.

  • Growth that waits on IT.

    A new branch, a new till system, online bookings. Each plan stalls because no one owns the technology side of it.

What changes when the seat is filled.

  • One person is accountable.

    You stop refereeing suppliers. ClearSeat manages them on your behalf and holds each one to what they promised in writing.

  • You can see what you have and what it costs.

    Every system, contract, renewal date and monthly cost on one page, with the waste and the risks marked.

  • Decisions get made properly.

    Before you sign for a new system or a three-year contract, someone on your side of the table has read it and asked the hard questions.

  • Plans get delivered.

    New branches, new systems and changes of supplier are run as projects, with a date, a budget and an owner.

How it works.

  1. A 30-minute call

    You describe the business and what is frustrating you. We tell you honestly whether we can help.

  2. The IT Health Check

    Three weeks, fixed fee. We map every system, supplier, contract and cost, and test the basics.

  3. A one-page plan

    What to fix first, what to stop paying for, and what it will take. Yours to keep, whatever you decide next.

  4. The seat, filled

    If you want us to carry it out, ClearSeat becomes your part-time IT leader for a flat monthly fee.
